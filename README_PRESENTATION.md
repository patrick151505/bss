# Barangay Smart System

> **The Barangay Smart System — a complete digital office for the barangay.** One secure system that replaces scattered logbooks, folders, and spreadsheets — so every resident record, certificate, incident, event, and peso of the budget lives in one place, searchable in seconds.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Most barangays still run on paper: handwritten logbooks, filing cabinets of certificates, budget tracked in notebooks, resident lists in loose sheets. This means:

- **Slow service** — residents wait while staff dig through folders.
- **Lost or duplicated records** — no single source of truth.
- **Hard to audit** — no clear trail of who did what and when.
- **No visibility** — officials can't see, at a glance, what's happening in their barangay.

**The Barangay Smart System turns all of that into a single, modern web system** that any staff member can use from a browser — fast, organized, and secure.

## Core Modules (What the System Can Do)

### 1. 👥 Citizen & Resident Records
The heart of the system — a complete, searchable registry of every resident.
- Full resident profiles with photo, address, and family links.
- Flags for **Voters, PWD, Senior Citizens, and Minors**.
- **Household & Family grouping** — see who lives together and family relationships.
- Instant search across the whole population.
- Approval workflow so new records are verified before they go live.
- **Demographics dashboard** — population insights at a glance.
- One-click **export** to spreadsheets for reports.

### 2. 📄 Document & Certificate Generation
Issue official barangay documents in seconds instead of typing them by hand.
- Ready-to-use **certificate templates** (Clearance, Indigency, Residency, and more) — fully customizable.
- **Auto-fill** resident details straight from their record — no re-typing.
- Request → **Approve → Release** workflow so nothing gets issued without sign-off.
- Tracks **purpose, amount paid, and print count** for every document.
- Records **who requested and who approved** each certificate.
- Print-ready output with the official barangay layout and captain's signature.

### 3. 🪪 Citizen ID Cards
Design and print professional barangay ID cards.
- Built-in **ID template designer** — customize the layout and background.
- Auto-generates IDs with resident photo and **QR code**.
- Upload signatures and print ready-to-laminate cards.

### 4. 📋 Blotter / Incident Records
A proper digital logbook for barangay complaints and incidents.
- Log incidents with parties involved, details, and photos.
- Track case **status and actions taken** over time.
- Record outcomes and resolutions — a full case history.
- Nothing gets lost; every case is searchable.

### 5. 🎪 Events & Attendance (with QR Check-in)
Run barangay events smoothly and know exactly who attended.
- Create events and manage attendee lists.
- **QR-code check-in** for fast, accurate attendance.
- **Manual and bulk add** options for flexibility.
- Live attendance dashboard.
- **Raffle draw feature** with a public audience screen — perfect for fiestas and assemblies.

### 6. 💰 Budget Management (Full Financial Control)
A serious, government-grade budgeting module built for accountability.
- **Fiscal year** setup with income estimates and program allocations.
- Track spending against budget line items in real time.
- Generate **vouchers** (Disbursement, Petty Cash, Payroll).
- Manage **suppliers, accountable officers, cash advances, and liquidation reports**.
- Configurable statutory thresholds per barangay.
- Complete **audit log** of every financial action.

### 7. 📦 Inventory Management
Track barangay property and supplies from stock-in to release.
- Organize items by **category**.
- **Stock-in and stock adjustment** with running balances.
- **Release requests** with an Approve → Release workflow and printable slips.
- Never lose track of equipment and supplies again.

### 8. 🎂 Birthdays & Community Engagement
- Automatic **birthday list** of residents — great for greetings and outreach.
- Exportable for programs and celebrations.

## Built-In Security & Accountability

This is what turns a "nice tool" into a **trustworthy government system**:

- 🔐 **Secure login** — only authorized staff get in.
- 👤 **Roles & Permissions** — each staff member sees only what their role allows (e.g., a clerk can't touch the budget).
- 📜 **Activity Logs** — every important action is recorded: who did it, what they changed, and when. Full audit trail.
- ✅ **Approval workflows** — sensitive actions (documents, releases, budget) require sign-off before they take effect.

## How It Works (Simple Flow for a Slide)

```
Staff logs in securely
        ↓
Opens the Dashboard (everything in one place)
        ↓
Picks a module → Citizens · Documents · Blotter · Events · Budget · Inventory
        ↓
Does the task (issue certificate, log incident, check-in guest, record expense…)
        ↓
System auto-fills, validates, and logs the action
        ↓
Approval where needed → Printed / Released / Recorded
        ↓
Everything saved, searchable, and auditable
```
